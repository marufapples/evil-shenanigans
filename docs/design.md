# Manga Tracker: Design Doc

## Problem
I am someone that loves to read manga, manhwa, manhua, and all sorts of different comic variations from around the world. As such, I've read a plethora of different pieces that I track through my history, across sites, on social medias, and honestly everything is scattered in random places that I lose track of. The main cause of this is the lack of a library that contains all the pieces I've read. 

I am aiming to resolve this by creating a tracker, similar to AniList and MyAnimeList, but contains a proper library and actually updates constantly without much interaction with the site so that all your mangas are up to date and readily accessible. 

## Goals
- Sync my reading list and progress from Anilist
- Create a way to automatically sync progress from external reading sites, or at least simplify the update process
- Add notifiers for new chapters on what I'm currently reading
